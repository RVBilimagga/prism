# User Stories

## FR-FINREQ-001
As a **business_analyst**, I want "classify requirements across business, technical, security, privacy, compliance, performance, availability, auditability, and operational dimensions" so that To ensure that all aspects of the requirements are considered and addressed appropriately..

**Acceptance criteria:**
- Requirements are classified into at least 9 categories: business, technical, security, privacy, compliance, performance, availability, auditability, and operational dimensions.

## FR-FINREQ-002
As a **business_analyst**, I want "map them to applicable financial regulations, organisational policies, risk controls, and legacy-system constraints" so that To ensure that all requirements are aligned with relevant laws, internal guidelines, and existing systems..

**Acceptance criteria:**
- Requirements are mapped to at least 3 categories: financial regulations, organisational policies, risk controls, and legacy-system constraints.

## FR-FINREQ-003
As a **business_analyst**, I want "generate a justified, project-specific SDLC workflow with phases, activities, roles, deliverables, validation gates, security controls, and compliance checkpoints" so that To ensure that the development process is structured and aligned with best practices for software development in financial services..

**Acceptance criteria:**
- A project-specific SDLC workflow includes at least 5 phases: planning, design, implementation, testing, and maintenance.

## FR-FINREQ-004
As a **business_analyst**, I want "classify requirements into business requirements, stakeholder requirements, functional requirements, security requirements, privacy requirements, regulatory requirements, performance requirements, availability and reliability requirements, usability requirements, data-management requirements, integration requirements, audit and reporting requirements, operational and maintenance requirements" so that To ensure that all aspects of the requirements are categorized appropriately for proper development and testing..

**Acceptance criteria:**
- Requirements are classified into at least 13 categories: business requirements, stakeholder requirements, functional requirements, security requirements, privacy requirements, regulatory requirements, performance requirements, availability and reliability requirements, usability requirements, data-management requirements, integration requirements, audit and reporting requirements, operational and maintenance requirements.

## FR-FINREQ-005
As a **business_analyst**, I want "generate requirement artefacts such as Software Requirements Specification (SRS), user stories, use cases, acceptance criteria, compliance mappings, risk register, traceability matrix, and recommended SDLC model" so that To ensure that all requirements are documented in a structured format for review and approval by stakeholders and team members..

**Acceptance criteria:**
- Requirement artefacts include at least 7 types: Software Requirements Specification (SRS), user stories, use cases, acceptance criteria, compliance mappings, risk register, traceability matrix.

## FR-FINREQ-006
As a **business_analyst**, I want "derive project characteristics such as requirement stability, regulatory criticality, security risk, project complexity, system size, legacy-system dependence, frequency of expected changes, need for continuous delivery, availability of stakeholders, testing and documentation requirements, budget and schedule constraints, need for formal verification, consequences of system failure" so that To ensure that the selected SDLC approach is appropriate for the specific project needs..

**Acceptance criteria:**
- Project characteristics include at least 10 factors: requirement stability, regulatory criticality, security risk, project complexity, system size, legacy-system dependence, frequency of expected changes, need for continuous delivery, availability of stakeholders, testing and documentation requirements.

## FR-FINREQ-007
As a **business_analyst**, I want "generate a ranked recommendation for an appropriate Software Development Life Cycle model based on the derived project characteristics" so that To ensure that the selected SDLC approach is aligned with the specific needs of the project..

**Acceptance criteria:**
- A ranked recommendation includes at least 3 approaches: Waterfall, Agile, DevSecOps hybrid.

## FR-FINREQ-008
As a **business_analyst**, I want "customise the selected SDLC with development phases, activities within each phase, responsible roles, expected deliverables, security activities, testing requirements, compliance checkpoints, human-approval gates, entry and exit criteria, traceability requirements" so that To ensure that the development process is structured and aligned with best practices for software development in financial services..

**Acceptance criteria:**
- SDLC customisation includes at least 5 phases: planning, design, implementation, testing, maintenance.

## FR-FINREQ-009
As a **business_analyst**, I want "implement human-in-the-loop controls for final requirement baselines, regulatory interpretations, high-risk security requirements, conflicting stakeholder decisions, architecture-critical requirements, SDLC selection, changes to approved requirements, production-readiness decisions" so that To ensure that the development process is transparent and accountable, with human oversight where necessary..

**Acceptance criteria:**
- Human-in-the-loop controls include at least 5 types of approvals: final requirement baselines, regulatory interpretations, high-risk security requirements, conflicting stakeholder decisions, architecture-critical requirements.

## FR-FINREQ-010
As a **business_analyst**, I want "secure the Agentic AI platform with role-based access control, multi-factor authentication, encryption in transit and at rest, sensitive-data masking, secure prompt and output filtering, agent-level permissions, retrieval-source allowlisting, protection against prompt injection, session isolation, audit logging, data-retention policies, model and knowledge-base versioning" so that To ensure that the development process is secure and compliant with relevant regulations..

**Acceptance criteria:**
- Platform security includes at least 10 controls: role-based access control, multi-factor authentication, encryption in transit and at rest, sensitive-data masking, secure prompt and output filtering, agent-level permissions, retrieval-source allowlisting, protection against prompt injection, session isolation, audit logging.
