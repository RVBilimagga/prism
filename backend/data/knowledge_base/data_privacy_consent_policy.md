Lenders must obtain explicit, informed consent from a customer before
collecting personal or financial data, and must use that data only for the
purpose stated at the time of consent. Consent must be recorded with a
timestamp and a reference to the exact consent text shown to the customer.

Customers have the right to withdraw consent and request erasure of their
data, subject to the lender's statutory record-retention obligations for
completed loan and payment records (see the Audit & Reporting Policy).

Sensitive personal data (national ID numbers, biometric data, account
numbers) must be encrypted at rest and in transit, and must be masked in
any system log, analytics export, or AI/LLM-facing interface.

Customer data must not be transferred to a third party or a jurisdiction
outside the approved processing region without a documented data-sharing
agreement and, where applicable, the customer's separate explicit consent.
