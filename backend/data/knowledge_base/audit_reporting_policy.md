All loan origination, disbursement, and repayment records must be retained
for a minimum of seven years after loan closure to support audit and
regulatory inspection, even if the customer has requested erasure of other,
non-statutory data (see the Data Privacy & Consent Policy).

The system must be able to reconstruct, for any given loan application, the
full decision trail: every requirement, input, agent or system decision,
human approval, and generated document that led to the outcome.

Suspicious transactions above the regulatory reporting threshold, or
matching a defined fraud-typology pattern, must be flagged to the
compliance team within one business day and must not be disclosed to the
customer involved ("tipping off") pending investigation.

Regulatory reports must be generated in the format and on the schedule
specified by the relevant regulator, and every report must be reviewable
and sign-off-able by an authorised compliance officer before submission.
