All new customers must complete identity verification before any account or
loan application is created. Acceptable identity documents are a government
photo ID and one proof of address dated within the last three months.

The system shall not permit a customer to submit a loan application until
KYC status is "verified". A customer whose KYC checks fail twice must be
routed to manual review by a compliance officer; automated re-submission
beyond two attempts is not permitted.

Politically exposed persons (PEPs) and customers flagged by sanctions-list
screening require enhanced due diligence and explicit sign-off from a
compliance officer before onboarding can complete.

Every KYC decision (verified, rejected, escalated) must be logged with a
timestamp, the identity of the reviewing system or officer, and the
evidence considered, to support audit and regulatory inspection.
