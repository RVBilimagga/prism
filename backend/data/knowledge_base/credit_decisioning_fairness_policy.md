Automated credit-decisioning logic must not use a customer's protected
characteristics (religion, caste, gender, marital status, disability) as a
direct or effective proxy input, and the criteria used for approval,
rejection, or pricing must be explainable to the customer on request.

Every credit decision — approve, reject, or refer — must be traceable to
the specific inputs, model version, and threshold that produced it. A
rejected applicant must receive the principal reason(s) for rejection in
writing (a Key Fact Statement) before the loan contract stage.

A human credit officer must review and may override any automated decision
above a defined loan-amount threshold, or any decision flagged by the
fraud-detection or fairness-monitoring subsystem as anomalous. Overrides
must be logged with the officer's justification.

Loan disbursement must be made directly to a bank account held in the
verified borrower's name; disbursement through an intermediary or
third-party account is not permitted without documented, case-specific
compliance approval.
